## What it does

Warranty Claim Triager reads incoming claims (email, portal, ERP export), checks coverage and entitlement, scores fraud risk, and routes each claim to approve / deny / review — with a full audit trail.

## Key features

- **Entitlement checks** — serial, warranty term and coverage validated automatically.
- **Fraud signals** — duplicate claims, tampered receipts and anomaly patterns flagged.
- **Smart routing** — approve / deny / human-review queues with SLA timers.
- **RMA handoff** — approved claims flow straight into RMA and logistics.

## Who it's for

Manufacturers, distributors and field-service organizations drowning in manual warranty review.
