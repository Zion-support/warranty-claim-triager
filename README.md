# Warranty Claim Triager

AI classification and auto-adjudication routing of warranty claims. Part of the **Zion AI App Network** — 100+ interlinked AI business tools by [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com).

## What it does

- Classifies incoming warranty claims by product, defect type and coverage terms
- Auto-adjudicates clear-cut claims; routes edge cases to the right reviewer
- Detects duplicate and fraudulent claims across channels
- Feeds claim outcomes back into quality and RMA analytics
